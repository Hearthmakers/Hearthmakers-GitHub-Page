Hearthmakers

Light the fire. Raise a home.

A voxel game made as a tribute and homage to Stonehearth. Play one pioneer, raise a home in a realm of your own and build it with friends, right in your browser.

About the game

Every home begins with a fire. In Hearthmakers each player is one pioneer, moved by point and click, who lights a fire in a realm of their own and raises a home around it: gathering, digging, crafting and building, block by block.

The world is a great open book. Realms live on a server, on its pages, beside public places that everyone shares. Invite friends in, meet others out in the world, form a party and trade what you have made. Turning to another world turns the page.

What you do

Your pioneer

  • One pioneer per player: click where to go, or steer with the keys
  • Strength, Agility, Vitality, Intelligence and Dexterity, and points that can always come back
  • Health, stamina, running, experience and levels
  • 22 hairstyles, 7 beards, skin, eyes, and clothes in two dyes of your choice

Your realm

  • A realm of your own, raised from the biome you choose
  • Decide who may enter, and what each role may do
  • Invite friends, or leave it open to visitors
  • Public places, open to everyone on the server

Gather and dig

  • Fell trees and break boulders with your tool belt
  • Dig, fill and level the voxel terrain, tunnels included
  • Dig up clay and fire it into bricks

Blueprints

  • A real editor: walls, rooms, floors, stairs and roofs
  • Stone, dirt, wood and brick, in many colours and 30 patterns
  • 44 doors and windows to set into your walls
  • Copy, paste, turn and mirror whole parts
  • Set a blueprint in your realm and it rises block by block

Craft and colonists

  • Workbenches, each calling a colonist of its profession: builder, farmer or guard
  • Colonists take tasks, patrol, tend farms and wear the gear you give them
  • A storage for the whole realm
  • They grow with their work

Fight and grow

  • Entlings and little stone golems roam the realms: click to fight
  • Weapons and armor that show on your pioneer
  • Experience and levels; if you fall, you rise again at home

Together

  • Chat with channels, commands and @ pings
  • Parties that last across sessions
  • Trades, with both offers side by side
  • Friends, friend requests and blocks
  • A portal for your characters, realms and blueprints

Three biomes

Temperate

Green terraces, oaks, pines and brown rock. A gentle place for a first home.

Desert

Pale sand and stone, cacti and flat-topped trees, with plenty of room under the sun.

Arctic

Deep snow, blue cliffs and golden trees. Keep close to the fire.

Made here

Hearthmakers started out on Stonehearth’s art, as placeholders. That migration is finished: the models, icons, sounds, cursors and windows you see are now the game’s own, made with tools built for it, while Stonehearth stays where it belongs, as the inspiration.

Models

Trees, rocks, crops, benches, tools, weapons, armor and the pioneers themselves: voxel models built by recipes, in one palette and one style, on the game’s own skeletons.

Sounds

Axes, picks, hoes, buttons and the chat’s ping, synthesized from maths with no recording behind them.

Interface

Every board, sign and sheet of parchment is painted by code. Even this page: the panels around this text come from the game’s own painter.

Questions

Is the game finished?

No. Hearthmakers is at version 0.0.1 and changes often. The Discord is the place to follow it.

Is this Stonehearth?

No. It is a free, fan-made tribute and homage to Stonehearth, with its own art, made by The Gearfinder Studios. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the makers of Stonehearth.

Do I need an account?

Yes: your characters and realms are kept on the server. You sign in with a username and password or, in the Discord Activity, with Discord.

What does it run on?

A modern browser on a computer, or a phone or tablet held in landscape.
