About the game

In Hearthmakers each player is a single hearthling, moved by point and click. Your hearthling lives in a realm of its own, raised from a biome, where you gather, dig, build and craft.

Realms live on a server, together with public places that everyone shares. Invite friends to your realm, meet others out in the world, form a party and trade what you have made.

Features

Your hearthling

One hearthling per player, moved by point and click, with attributes, health, stamina and gear.

Realms and places

Personal realms on a server, with invites for friends, and public places open to everyone. Each realm rises from its biome: temperate, desert or arctic.

Harvest and dig

Fell trees and break boulders with your tool belt, and dig, fill and level the voxel terrain, tunnels included.

Blueprints

Draw structures in stone, dirt, wood, clay and bricks, with walls and building patterns, then build them in your realm block by block.

Crafting and colonists

Workbenches and colonists who work by them, take on tasks, grow farms, patrol and dress for their profession.

Combat and levels

Fight entlings and stone golems, earn experience and level up.

Play together

Chat with channels and commands, parties, trades and friends that stay across sessions.

Touch screens too

Every feature also works on touch screens in landscape.
